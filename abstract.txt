ABSTRACT (100 words)

This personal website introduces my background as a Bachelor of Information Technology with Business Management student. It is designed to present my education, personal interests, professional interests, learning activities and career ambitions in one organised place. The website also highlights selected coursework and technology projects. Overall, it serves as a simple personal portfolio for academic and future career purposes.
